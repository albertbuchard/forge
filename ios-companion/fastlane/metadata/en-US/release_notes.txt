Forge Companion 1.0.143 finishes the watch sync transport wording cleanup and keeps the release retry hardening from 1.0.142.

Tailscale watch connections now present themselves as direct HTTPS/Tailscale routes in the watch Sync surface, instead of using relay-style wording that made a Tailscale pairing look mixed with another transport.

When watchOS cannot reach Forge directly, the UI now calls that path a phone fallback. It no longer says queued action, relay, or iPhone relay in the normal watch sync flow or in the iPhone coverage details.

The durable fallback still preserves offline actions until Forge accepts them, but direct Tailscale remains the preferred path whenever watchOS can reach the secure companion URL.

The Tailscale/Iroh separation remains in place: secure Tailscale URLs stay on the Tailscale path, and Iroh is only used for active forge-iroh pairings.

Release automation now reconciles Apple transporter accepted-upload 500s and picks a build number above the latest App Store Connect build.

HealthKit cursors, upload checkpoints, pairing tokens, watch action queues, and stored user data are unchanged.
