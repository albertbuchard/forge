Forge Companion 1.0.66 fixes stale HealthKit chunk checkpoints that could keep
automatic syncs cycling through cancelled attempts.

The app now refuses to resume old base-health chunk checkpoints from previous
builds, so protected-data-empty sleep chunks cannot block later real sleep data.

If Forge reports missing required HealthKit chunks during finalization, the app
now aborts that stale upload session and starts the next retry from a clean
session instead of preserving the broken checkpoint.

Base HealthKit chunks now use payload-addressed identities, preventing a real
sleep, vitals, or movement chunk from being skipped because an earlier empty
payload used the same sequence number.
