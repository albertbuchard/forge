Forge Companion 1.0.35 makes pairing dramatically easier away from a local network.

Forge now creates tunnel-first pairing sessions by default, so the iPhone app can use
a recreated, token-bound endpoint instead of depending on a fragile LAN, direct TCP,
or Tailscale-only path.

Manual HTTP pairing is still available for advanced local setups, and the app now
preserves the transport metadata from QR codes and discovery advertisements so it can
prefer the strongest route automatically.
