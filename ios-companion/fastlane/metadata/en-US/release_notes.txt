Forge Companion 1.0.106 makes HealthKit sync progress easier to trust.

The sync panel now separates phone-side preparation from network upload time. When the iPhone is gathering HealthKit payloads or preparing workout evidence, Forge shows that local preparation instead of reporting a stale "since last Forge reply" timer.

Recent and historical workout imports now publish preparation telemetry before each evidence batch, so long gaps are easier to diagnose as HealthKit gathering, chunk preparation, or Forge upload/processing.
