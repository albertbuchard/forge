Forge Companion 1.0.127 finishes the Tailscale/Iroh transport separation and tightens direct sync behavior.

New Tailscale pairings now produce a direct Tailscale payload without Iroh metadata. Existing mixed pairings are normalized on the phone so a Tailscale HTTPS URL is treated as Tailscale direct for sync, chunking, progress labels, and WebView routing.

Foreground Tailscale uploads now use the shorter direct-transfer timeout instead of waiting on the old long fallback path, so stalled requests fail and resume sooner while background uploads keep their system-friendly timeout.

The pairing screen copy now matches the actual policy: Tailscale/LAN direct when reachable, Iroh only for Iroh pairings.

HealthKit cursors, upload checkpoints, pairing state, and stored user data are unchanged.
