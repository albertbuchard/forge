Forge Companion 1.0.51 improves large HealthKit workout backfills.

Workout archive chunks are now processed by Forge as they arrive, so synced
workouts can appear in the Sports browser before the full upload session
finishes.

The Sports browser now receives a lightweight workout list instead of being
limited to the 40-row analytics summary window.

Background sync scheduling now logs whether iOS accepted the refresh and
processing requests, and unfinished HealthKit upload sessions are prioritized
for resume when iOS grants background execution.
