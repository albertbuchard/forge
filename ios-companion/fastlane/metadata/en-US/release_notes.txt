Forge Companion 1.0.48 makes large HealthKit backfills use far fewer
transfers and keeps active uploads alive when the app moves to the background.

Workout time-series and route data are now packed into larger byte-stable
chunks instead of one tiny upload per workout, and Forge accepts compressed
chunk payloads so large heart-rate backfills transfer less data.

Manual syncs now hold the active sync task during the iOS background window,
while scheduled app-refresh and processing tasks continue to resume accepted
chunks when iOS grants background time.

The sync screen still shows live upload counts, payload size, current transfer
family, and copyable logs so stalled or slow syncs are diagnosable.
