Forge Companion 1.0.152 tightens watchOS direct sync and paired-iPhone backup behavior for Tailscale pairings.

When the watch gets a live Forge acknowledgement for an action that was also saved in the paired-iPhone backup queue, the phone now removes that acknowledged duplicate from its durable relay queue. This prevents already-accepted watch actions from being replayed later by the phone backup path.

The watch Sync screen also uses clearer wording: direct HTTPS is shown as the primary path, and the paired iPhone is described only as backup when direct sync cannot run.

Pairing tokens, upload checkpoints, HealthKit data, watch action persistence, and stored user data are unchanged.
