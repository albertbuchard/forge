Forge Companion 1.0.138 batches reachable watch actions through the iPhone relay.

When the watch cannot reach Forge directly but the iPhone is reachable, multiple pending watch actions now travel in one WatchConnectivity request and one Forge command batch instead of one request per action.

This reduces phone-relay chatter for bursts of habit, task, and capture actions while keeping the same durable retry path when the iPhone is offline or cannot submit to Forge.

The relay protocol also remains compatible with older single-action watch acknowledgements during rollout.

The Tailscale/Iroh separation remains in place: secure Tailscale URLs stay on the Tailscale path, and Iroh is only used for active forge-iroh pairings.

HealthKit cursors, upload checkpoints, pairing tokens, watch action queues, and stored user data are unchanged.
