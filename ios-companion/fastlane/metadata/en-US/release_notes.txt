Forge Companion 1.0.128 improves the watchOS companion refresh path for Tailscale and direct Forge connections.

The watch now tries a secure direct Forge refresh first when the paired endpoint is a reachable HTTPS URL such as Tailscale. The iPhone relay is kept only as a fallback when the watch cannot reach Forge directly.

Watch copy now describes the real path: direct Forge when available, iPhone relay only when needed. Stale “iPhone bridge” and “waiting for iPhone” messages were removed from the watch surfaces.

Regression coverage was added so Tailscale HTTPS is accepted as a direct watch route, while loopback, plain HTTP, and logical Iroh URLs are rejected for watch URLSession direct networking.

HealthKit cursors, upload checkpoints, pairing state, and stored user data are unchanged.
