Forge Companion 1.0.108 keeps foreground HealthKit uploads moving more aggressively.

If a HealthKit sync starts in the background and you open the app, Forge now switches active HTTP chunk uploads back to the faster foreground URLSession path instead of staying stuck on the one-at-a-time background transfer lane.

Foregrounded HTTP syncs can use the full prepared-chunk upload window again while keeping conservative background-sized chunk boundaries for resume safety.
