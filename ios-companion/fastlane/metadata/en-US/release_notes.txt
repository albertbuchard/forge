Forge Companion 1.0.130 tightens live sync status during staged HealthKit uploads.

The sync card no longer keeps showing an old completed message such as “Synced 22 workouts” while a new upload stage is still preparing. It now says that the next upload is being prepared until real chunk telemetry is available.

This builds on the previous Tailscale/Iroh fixes: Tailscale pairings remain labeled as Tailscale, Iroh is only used for Iroh pairings, and the progress text separates phone-side sending from Forge-side acceptance.

Regression coverage was added for stale completed sync messages during active uploads.

HealthKit cursors, upload checkpoints, pairing state, and stored user data are unchanged.
