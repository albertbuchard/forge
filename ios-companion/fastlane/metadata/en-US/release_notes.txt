Forge Companion 1.0.35 makes pairing dramatically easier away from a local network.

Forge now creates Iroh pairing sessions by default, so the iPhone app can use
a Rust-backed QUIC endpoint instead of depending on a fragile LAN, direct TCP, or
Tailscale-only path.

Manual HTTP pairing is still available for advanced local setups, and the app now links
the native Rust Iroh bridge so QR codes can carry the real node id, relay hint, ALPN,
and pairing token.
