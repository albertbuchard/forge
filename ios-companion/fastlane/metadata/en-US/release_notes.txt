Forge Companion 1.0.81 fixes the watchOS companion data path so surfaces are
fed by Forge backend snapshots instead of empty placeholder cards.

Habits, Psyche, and Work now use horizontal card paging inside each surface.
Habits can be marked from the Watch, Work shows individual tasks from the
Kanban snapshot, and Psyche questions are derived from Forge's backend event,
emotion, value, behavior, and mode definitions.

Psyche choices now project back into Forge as canonical self-observation notes
or trigger reports, so Watch entries are visible in Forge rather than staying
as local-only captures.

The iPhone relay now refreshes backend snapshots for valid paired watches even
when the pairing predates the newer watch-ready capability, preserves the last
good snapshot while pairing is warming up, and retries queued Watch actions
until Forge confirms them.
