Forge Companion 1.0.133 improves watch sync clarity and keeps transport labels tied to the active route.

The watch sync page now shows whether it has a direct Forge route, how many local actions are still pending, and whether it is sending directly through Tailscale or falling back to the iPhone relay.

Watch action status now says "Sending to Forge through Tailscale" when a secure Tailscale URL is available, instead of vague queued or relay wording.

The Tailscale/Iroh route rules are unchanged: secure Tailscale URLs use direct networking, while loopback, plain HTTP, and logical Iroh URLs are rejected for direct watch calls and use the relay path instead.

HealthKit cursors, upload checkpoints, pairing state, watch queues, and stored user data are unchanged.
