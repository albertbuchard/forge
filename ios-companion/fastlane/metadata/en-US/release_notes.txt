Forge Companion 1.0.65 fixes the HealthKit workout import getting stuck after a
background expiration or stalled route upload.

When iOS locks protected HealthKit data, the app now pauses the unfinished
import and waits for unlock instead of repeatedly starting a sync that cannot
read workouts.

When iOS expires the background task, the active sync is cancelled cleanly and
Forge resumes from the last accepted backend chunk on the next run.

Workout evidence uploads now use smaller batches and ordered chunk uploads, so
one failing route chunk stops immediately instead of leaving dozens of
background URLSession uploads queued behind a dead task.

The upload watchdog now detects a sync with no accepted chunk for five minutes,
clears the spinner, and leaves the manual Sync button able to restart from the
backend checkpoint.
