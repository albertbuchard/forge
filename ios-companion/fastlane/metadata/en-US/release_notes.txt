Forge Companion 1.0.147 makes watch direct-sync status evidence-based instead of configuration-based.

The Sync surface now says when a Tailscale/HTTPS route is only configured, when the watch has verified direct Forge reachability, and when direct watch delivery failed.

If the paired iPhone backup sends an action after direct watch delivery fails, the watch now says that explicitly instead of implying that Tailscale direct sync succeeded.

Direct watch failures now show the concrete error on the watch so Tailscale reachability problems are visible instead of looking like a stuck sync.

The iPhone HealthKit sync path, Tailscale/Iroh transport selection rules, pairing tokens, upload checkpoints, watch action persistence, and stored user data are unchanged.

Existing route tests still verify that a Tailscale HTTPS pairing does not use Iroh metadata, and that Iroh is used only for active forge-iroh pairings.
