Forge Companion 1.0.70 forces historical workout heart-rate and route evidence
to be replayed with the latest evidence contract.

Forge no longer accepts older sparse workout evidence versions as complete, so
opening the app can trigger a real historical workout evidence repair instead of
silently skipping already-uploaded workout summaries.

The historical import progress panels from 1.0.69 remain visible on the main
paired screen, top-right menu, and Settings while this repair runs.
