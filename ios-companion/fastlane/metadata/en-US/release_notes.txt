Forge Companion 1.0.113 keeps more foreground HealthKit upload chunks prepared while network requests are already in flight.

Foreground HTTP sync now maintains two prepared upload windows, so the next HealthKit chunks are ready as soon as Forge replies instead of waiting for the app to prepare the next chunk after each response.

The sync panel keeps the clearer Forge reply wording from the previous build, avoiding the old “ack” label while the phone is waiting on active upload responses.
