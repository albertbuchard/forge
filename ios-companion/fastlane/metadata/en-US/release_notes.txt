Forge Companion 1.0.145 makes foreground Tailscale and direct HTTPS HealthKit uploads more aggressive and clearer.

Foreground direct uploads now keep up to 12 chunk requests in flight instead of 8, with the direct URLSession connection cap raised to match. Iroh foreground uploads remain capped separately at 8, and background uploads remain serial for iOS background reliability.

The prepared direct upload queue now holds 36 chunks instead of 24, so the phone can keep more HealthKit evidence ready while Tailscale is accepting previous chunks.

The sync panel now describes active work as requests in flight and network responses, not vague replies or ack-style wording. It also keeps Tailscale direct labels separate from Iroh labels.

Existing route tests still verify that a Tailscale HTTPS pairing does not use Iroh metadata, and that Iroh is used only for active forge-iroh pairings.

HealthKit cursors, upload checkpoints, pairing tokens, watch action persistence, and stored user data are unchanged.
