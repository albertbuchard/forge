Forge Companion 1.0.139 makes manual watch sync retries use the direct secure route immediately.

When the watch has a secure HTTPS/Tailscale companion URL and the user taps Refresh or Retry, the watch now retries the direct route immediately instead of waiting behind the previous transient-failure cooldown.

Automatic direct-route retry cooldown is reduced from 45 seconds to 20 seconds, so temporary network misses recover sooner without repeatedly hammering Forge.

The iPhone relay remains a durable fallback for offline or unreachable watches, and previously queued actions are preserved until Forge accepts them.

The Tailscale/Iroh separation remains in place: secure Tailscale URLs stay on the Tailscale path, and Iroh is only used for active forge-iroh pairings.

HealthKit cursors, upload checkpoints, pairing tokens, watch action queues, and stored user data are unchanged.
