Forge Companion 1.0.36 switches pairing to Forge's own Rust Iroh/QUIC transport.

New QR codes carry a Forge node id, relay hint, ALPN forge-companion/1, and pairing
token so the app can connect through the native Forge transport instead of depending on
Tailscale or a fragile local-network address.

Manual HTTP pairing remains available for advanced local setups.
