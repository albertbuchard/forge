Forge Companion 1.0.35 keeps pairing alive more reliably.

The app now saves Forge's refreshed pairing expiry after verification, heartbeat,
sync, movement bootstrap, and source-state updates, so a healthy paired phone should
not fall back into repeated re-pairing just because the original QR code expired.

It also keeps a lightweight heartbeat and periodic sync cadence active while the app
is open, schedules background refresh when iOS allows it, and starts a short refresh
window when the app leaves the foreground.
