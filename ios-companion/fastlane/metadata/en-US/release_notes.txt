Forge Companion 1.0.56 makes the one-time historical workout import clearer during sync.

Workout summaries now upload separately from raw HR, metric, and route evidence,
so Forge can show workout sessions and apply arriving evidence progressively
instead of waiting for the whole sync session to complete.

Historical workout import now has its own progress panel with found workouts,
uploaded workouts, HR and metric evidence, route points, transfer speed,
resumed chunks, and remaining workout count.

Full-history backfills still load the full workout set together while keeping
raw HR, metric, and route uploads intact.

Forge also processes workout time-series and route chunks as soon as they arrive,
so backend analytics can update during the upload rather than only after finalization.
