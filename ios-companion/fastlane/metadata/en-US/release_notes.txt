Forge Companion 1.0.95 fixes sleep timing freshness and HealthKit sync recovery.

Sleep views now use the sleeper's source timezone and local wake-date to decide what "last night" means, so stale synced nights are surfaced instead of mislabeled as current.

HealthKit sync now discards stale active checkpoints before starting a fresh normal sync, preventing an old upload window from completing later and hiding the newest sleep night.
