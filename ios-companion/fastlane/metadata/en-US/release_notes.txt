Forge Companion 1.0.79 improves the watchOS command surface and keeps the
historical HealthKit evidence replay fix from 1.0.78.

Historical workouts whose heart-rate, time-series, and route evidence was already
uploaded are no longer treated as missing just because they used an older raw
evidence contract. Truly missing evidence remains replayable, but completed
workouts should not restart the historical import on app launch.

The Watch app now opens into a clear Forge surface navigator: the Digital Crown
moves between Now, Work, Habits, Goals, Today, Health, Movement, Psyche, Inbox,
and Sync, while horizontal paging selects the current card inside each surface.
Every surface now has a fast action or logging path instead of leaving the user
stuck on one command page.

The Watch asks the iPhone to refresh and relay automatically when it opens or
regains connectivity. The iPhone now acknowledges only backend-confirmed watch
commands, keeps unacknowledged actions queued for retry, and applies fresh
snapshots from backend receipts.
