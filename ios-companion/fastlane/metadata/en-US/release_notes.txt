Forge Companion 1.0.62 makes HealthKit resume decisions backend-authoritative.

The iOS app now refreshes the Forge sync session status before base uploads and
before every workout batch, then skips only chunks the backend reports as already
accepted.

Workout summary, time-series, and route chunk ids are stable across restarts and
no longer depend on the sequence number remaining after a local checkpoint.

The local completed-workout checkpoint is no longer used to decide what should be
skipped; the backend’s accepted chunk list is the source of truth.

Workout chunks are queued through iOS background URLSession uploads by mapped
batch, so accepted chunks can continue to land while the app is suspended and are
reconciled from backend status after relaunch.

This TestFlight build keeps beta review submission disabled while CI uploads the
IPA, waits for processing, and attaches this changelog.
