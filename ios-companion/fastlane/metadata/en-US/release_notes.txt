Forge Companion 1.0.39 makes HealthKit sports sync resumable for large workout histories.

Workout backfills now upload to Forge in bounded chunks instead of one oversized request,
so raw heart-rate timelines, routes, workout summaries, sleep, vitals, movement, and
screen-time evidence can resume after a network drop or app backgrounding.

Sync progress and diagnostics now show the active upload session, chunk family, payload
size, and server limits, while Forge returns typed payload-too-large and checksum errors
instead of exposing raw server JSON.
