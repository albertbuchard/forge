Forge Companion 1.0.119 reduces foreground HealthKit sync round trips over the Iroh bridge and makes sync speed easier to read.

Foreground Iroh workout evidence chunks now target 2 MB instead of 1 MB. In the unit model for 100,000 workout time-series records, that cuts chunks from 65 to 32 and upload waves from 11 to 6 with the six-request foreground window.

The sync panel now separates phone-side queued speed from Forge-side stored speed, so long transport waits are not mislabeled as slow Forge processing. Background uploads remain serialized and durable.
