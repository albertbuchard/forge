Forge Companion 1.0.48 makes large HealthKit backfills start uploading sooner
and shows live transfer speed while the sync is running.

Workout history is now scanned recent-first in smaller HealthKit windows, so a
full backfill no longer has to count every old workout before the first upload
can begin.

The sync panel now reports current speed, average speed, accepted chunks,
uploaded bytes, and time since the last Forge acknowledgement. This makes slow
or stalled transfers visible without opening the logs.

Large workout payloads still use byte-stable chunking, compression, resume
support, and copyable logs for diagnosis.
