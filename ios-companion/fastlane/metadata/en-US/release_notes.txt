Forge Companion 1.0.67 backfills missing historical workout heart-rate and
route evidence instead of treating a previously uploaded workout summary as
complete evidence.

Workout evidence chunks now use payload-addressed identities, so a prior empty
or partial workout upload cannot block a later retry that includes the real
heart-rate samples or route points.

Forge now asks the app to replay legacy workouts until explicit evidence
metadata confirms heart-rate and route capture, so opening the app should repair
old kickboxing and workout evidence gaps during sync.
