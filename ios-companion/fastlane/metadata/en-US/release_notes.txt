Forge Companion 1.0.109 keeps a foreground HealthKit sync from staying stuck on an old background upload window.

If a workout evidence phase starts while the app is backgrounded and you open Forge, the chunk scheduler now re-checks the foreground upload window while the first slow chunk is still waiting for a Forge reply.

That means prepared workout chunks can start filling the faster foreground pipe within about a second instead of waiting for the first background-lane upload to finish.
