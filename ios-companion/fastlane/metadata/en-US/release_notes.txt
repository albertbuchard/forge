Forge Companion 1.0.134 fixes stale Tailscale/manual transport state and makes follow-up workout evidence uploads clearer.

Existing pairings that point at a secure Tailscale `*.ts.net` Forge URL are now normalized back to Tailscale mode even if an older payload still said manual HTTP.

The sync panel no longer shows a completed "Synced..." headline while a historical workout import or missing heart-rate/route evidence repair is active.

Historical workout evidence now has its own pipeline status so the app shows that Forge is preparing or importing workout evidence instead of looking stuck on the previous sync result.

HealthKit cursors, upload checkpoints, pairing tokens, watch action queues, and stored user data are unchanged.
