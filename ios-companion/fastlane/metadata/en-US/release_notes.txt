Forge Companion 1.0.88 fixes Iroh bridge timeout recovery after reconnecting or repairing.

Iroh remains the first sync path, but new pairings now keep the direct Forge API/UI URL as a fallback so the app can retry over HTTPS/Tailscale when the Iroh bridge times out.

Discovery and pairing metadata were also tightened so stale Iroh endpoints do not block a phone-reachable Forge server.
