Forge Companion 1.0.121 makes HealthKit sync progress easier to understand while uploads are in flight.

The sync panel now separates phone-side queueing, transport replies, and chunks accepted by Forge. It also shows the active upload window, such as 3/6 requests, so the tail of a batch is not mistaken for Forge blocking the upload.

Transport labels now say whether Iroh is primary and whether Tailscale is only a fallback. HealthKit cursors, upload checkpoints, pairing state, and upload behavior are unchanged.
