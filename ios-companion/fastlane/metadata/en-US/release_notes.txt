Forge Companion 1.0.146 keeps watch sync transport behavior aligned with the selected pairing route.

Watch actions now describe and retry direct secure Forge delivery more clearly. A Tailscale or HTTPS watch route is treated as the primary path, and the paired iPhone is shown only as a backup when the watch cannot reach Forge directly.

Recoverable direct watch failures now schedule a short direct retry after the cooldown instead of leaving the user staring at stale backup wording.

Watch sync and empty-state copy no longer uses queue, relay, or phone-fallback jargon for normal Tailscale sessions.

The iPhone HealthKit, movement, pairing tokens, upload checkpoints, watch action persistence, and stored user data are unchanged.

Existing route tests still verify that a Tailscale HTTPS pairing does not use Iroh metadata, and that Iroh is used only for active forge-iroh pairings.
