Forge Companion 1.0.53 removes slow local workout chunk preparation, reduces sync-time maintenance traffic, and resumes cleanly when HealthKit is locked.

Workout summaries now upload separately from raw HR, metric, and route evidence,
so Forge can show workout sessions and apply arriving evidence progressively
instead of waiting for the whole sync session to complete.

Workout evidence collection now uses bulk HealthKit range reads for each batch
instead of doing a full per-workout mapping pass on the phone. Full-history
backfills use smaller 20-workout batches so uploads start sooner and progress
updates stay active.

Forge also processes workout time-series and route chunks as soon as they arrive,
so backend analytics can update during the upload rather than only after finalization.
