Forge Companion 1.0.82 fixes HealthKit sync recovery when uploads are
interrupted before Forge accepts any chunks.

Interrupted sleep, movement, and historical workout uploads now reset cleanly
and retry instead of surfacing stale empty-session errors.

Historical workout import now treats an empty upload as complete only when the
Forge backend agrees all workout evidence is already present. Missing heart
rate, time-series, or route evidence still remains eligible for replay.

The Forge web view also ignores benign cancelled navigations so normal reloads
do not leave the companion status stuck on an error.
