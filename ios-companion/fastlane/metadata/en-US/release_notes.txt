Forge Companion 1.0.37 polishes the Forge QR pairing flow on iPhone.

The setup screen now leads with Scan Forge QR, keeps Manual connection as the fallback,
and explains the Forge Iroh/QUIC route without requiring a separate Tailscale app.

This build also ships through the corrected TestFlight release lane so processed builds
are distributed to the configured beta group instead of only being uploaded.
