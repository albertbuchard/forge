Forge Companion 1.0.120 removes a client-side bottleneck from foreground HealthKit sync over the Iroh bridge.

The embedded Iroh client runtime now has six worker threads to match the six-request foreground HealthKit upload window. That keeps concurrent workout evidence streams from being squeezed through the previous two-worker client runtime.

This release keeps the 2 MB foreground Iroh chunks and the clearer sync-speed panel from 1.0.119. HealthKit cursors, upload checkpoints, pairing state, and background upload behavior are unchanged.
