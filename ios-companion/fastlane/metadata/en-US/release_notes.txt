Forge Companion 1.0.69 restores the historical workout import progress view to
the main paired app surface, so heart-rate and route evidence repairs are visible
without digging through Settings.

The top-right companion menu now also shows a compact historical import card
whenever a historical workout repair is active, paused, waiting for unlock, or
recently completed.

This keeps the existing historical upload function intact while making missing
workout evidence replay explicit on-device.
