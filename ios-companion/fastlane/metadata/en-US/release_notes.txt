Forge Companion 1.0.58 fixes the one-time historical workout import stall during sync.

The app now counts the full workout library first, reports the total immediately,
then reads HealthKit workout evidence in smaller internal slices so uploads start
moving again without capping the historical import.

Historical workout import now has its own progress panel with found workouts,
uploaded workouts, HR and metric evidence, route points, transfer speed,
resumed chunks, and remaining workout count.

Workout summaries still upload separately from raw HR, metric, and route evidence,
so Forge can show workout sessions and apply arriving evidence progressively.

This TestFlight build uploads without automatic external group distribution, avoiding
App Store Connect submission-limit failures while keeping build processing and
changelog verification in CI.
