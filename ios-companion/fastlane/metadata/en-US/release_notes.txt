Forge Companion 1.0.148 tightens the watch direct-sync status so it cannot claim a route is ready before it is actually verified.

When the watch activates with a configured Tailscale HTTPS route, it now says it is testing that direct route until a real request proves Forge is reachable.

The watch no longer treats paired-session activation as proof that Tailscale direct sync works.

The iPhone HealthKit sync path, Tailscale/Iroh transport selection rules, pairing tokens, upload checkpoints, watch action persistence, and stored user data are unchanged.
