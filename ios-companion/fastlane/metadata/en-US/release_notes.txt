Forge Companion 1.0.78 fixes HealthKit historical workout evidence replay.

Historical workouts whose heart-rate, time-series, and route evidence was already
uploaded are no longer treated as missing just because they used an older raw
evidence contract. Truly missing evidence remains replayable, but completed
workouts should not restart the historical import on app launch.

This build also includes the Forge plugin/runtime 0.2.102 release and clearer
sync-state reporting for missing versus stale workout evidence.
