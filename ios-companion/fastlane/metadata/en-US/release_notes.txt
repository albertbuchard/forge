Forge Companion 1.0.71 keeps historical workout repair visible inside the native
iOS menu and Settings without covering the Forge web app.

The historical workout progress display now clamps replayed workout summaries to
the backend-reported total, so repaired sessions cannot show more uploaded
workouts than found.

The top-right native menu button is more visible during historical repair, and
the app continues using the current raw workout evidence contract for heart-rate
and route replay.
