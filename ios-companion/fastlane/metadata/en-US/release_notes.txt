Forge Companion 1.0.90 fixes Forge WebView loading over Iroh pairings.

The embedded Forge view now preserves the exact `/forge/` path and refresh query when proxying through `forge-iroh://`, avoiding the WebKit “Frame load interrupted” failure seen after successful sync.

The Iroh WebView bridge also now returns WebKit-safe responses, follows Forge redirects explicitly, and logs scheme-handler request/response details for easier diagnosis.
