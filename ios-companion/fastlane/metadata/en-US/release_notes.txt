Forge Companion 1.0.68 forces a historical workout evidence repair when Forge
reports uploaded workout summaries with missing heart-rate or route evidence,
even if the phone had previously marked the one-time backfill complete.

Forge now distinguishes summary-only historical rows from real zero-heart-rate
route-only workouts, so old kickboxing summaries are replayed until raw evidence
metadata confirms the heart-rate and route capture state.

Base HealthKit chunks use payload-addressed identities, so empty locked-device
sleep/vitals chunks cannot block a later retry that includes the real data.
