Forge Companion 1.0.114 keeps historical workout evidence preparation ahead of uploads instead of mapping one batch only after the previous upload finishes.

Historical workout imports now prefetch mapped workout batches inside each HealthKit scan window, helping the phone keep the upload pipeline fed while Forge is processing active requests.

The sync panel now separates accepted throughput, requests waiting on Forge, and phone-side preparation so long waits are easier to diagnose without the old “ack” wording.
