Forge Companion 1.0.76 ships the new Forge watchOS command surface and the
updated Forge plugin runtime.

The Watch app now uses a wrist-first control model: the Digital Crown selects
Forge surfaces, horizontal paging selects the current card, and tapping opens
compact actions for Now, Work, Habits, Goals, Today, Health, Movement, Psyche,
Inbox, and Sync.

The iPhone remains the secure relay for pairing, retry queues, backend calls,
and snapshot publishing, while the backend now supports idempotent batched watch
commands for habit check-ins, capture events, and task-run/task status actions.
