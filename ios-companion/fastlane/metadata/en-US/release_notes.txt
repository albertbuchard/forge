Forge Companion 1.0.38 expands HealthKit workout sync for the new Forge sports cockpit.

Workouts now send richer HealthKit evidence to Forge, including heart-rate timelines,
route points, workout events, activity phases, energy, distance, speed, cadence, power,
steps, swimming metrics, flights, and VO2 max samples when Health permissions allow it.

Heart-rate samples are linked to each workout first and then recovered by strict workout
time windows when HealthKit associations are missing, so older sessions can still produce
clear HR coverage, zone, and sync-quality states in Forge.
