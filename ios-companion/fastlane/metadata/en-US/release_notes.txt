Forge Companion 1.0.136 makes Tailscale sync status clearer and watch fallback faster.

Completed phone syncs now name the actual transport, such as Tailscale direct or Iroh primary, so a manual button press is not confused with manual transport.

The watch direct-route timeout is now 6 seconds instead of 12 seconds before falling back to the iPhone relay. That halves the worst-case wait when the watch cannot reach a secure Tailscale route directly.

The Tailscale/Iroh separation from 1.0.135 remains in place: secure Tailscale URLs stay on the Tailscale path, and Iroh is only used for active forge-iroh pairings.

HealthKit cursors, upload checkpoints, pairing tokens, watch action queues, and stored user data are unchanged.
