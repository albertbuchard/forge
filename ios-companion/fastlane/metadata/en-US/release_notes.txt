Forge Companion 1.0.73 ships the clearer Forge training-load and shell UI
release alongside the updated Forge plugin runtime.

Training Load now explains cardiovascular load, heart-rate zones, acute/chronic
balance, smart training modes, and next training targets with help affordances
directly beside the metrics and charts.

The mobile Forge shell separates the page title, action search, XP summary, and
work timer so the web app remains readable on small screens.
