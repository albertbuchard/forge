Forge Companion 1.0.50 fixes HealthKit workout backfill getting trapped on the
old slow upload path.

If the phone resumes a stale sync session that does not support compressed
workout archives, Forge now discards that stale session and starts a fresh one
instead of falling back to separate summary, time-series, and route uploads.

Workout backfills now send much larger batches, map more workouts in parallel,
and keep the compressed archive path as the required upload mode for workout
history. The app also registers a background processing task with network
connectivity so iOS has the correct long-running background contract for sync.
