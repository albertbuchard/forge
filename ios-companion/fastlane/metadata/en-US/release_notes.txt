Forge Companion 1.0.135 improves watch sync routing for secure Tailscale pairings.

The watch now only attempts direct API calls for secure, non-local URLs such as Tailscale HTTPS endpoints. Iroh URLs stay on the Iroh path; Tailscale URLs stay labeled as Tailscale.

If the watch cannot reach the direct route, it stops repeatedly waiting on the same network timeout and temporarily uses the iPhone relay instead.

The watch sync card now says Clear, Sending, or To send instead of Pending, so staged actions are easier to understand.

HealthKit cursors, upload checkpoints, pairing tokens, watch action queues, and stored user data are unchanged.
