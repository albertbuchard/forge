Forge Companion 1.0.84 fixes CLI QR pairing.

The iOS app now understands the shorter QR payload emitted by Forge Memory, scans all detected QR candidates before giving up, and logs malformed QR payloads instead of silently ignoring them.

Manual setup is more forgiving too: paste the saved payload from ~/.forge/pairing/, Forge Settings -> Mobile, or the full output from npx forge-memory pair-ios --json.
