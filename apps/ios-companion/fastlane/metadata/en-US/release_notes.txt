Forge Companion 1.0.171 adds Agent Messages, a native asynchronous inbox and outbox for sending text, a voice note, or both to a connected Forge agent.

Talk to agent starts recording immediately when microphone access is available, while text remains usable when it is not. Choose the default agent or another connected agent, then follow delivery, progress, acknowledgement, result, failure, forwarding, and history from the native mailbox.

Offline messages remain in an AES-GCM encrypted queue with a device-only Keychain key. Forge retries when connectivity and iOS execution time return, labels Wi-Fi and background waiting honestly, and never promises an Apple background deadline. Authorized notifications contain only the agent label and generic state, not message or transcript content.
