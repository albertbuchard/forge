Forge Companion 1.0.179 repairs HealthKit sync sessions that could remain stale after a delayed upload or a temporary Forge database lock.

Background transfers now use a replay-protected proof that remains valid when iOS defers delivery. A system-cancelled transfer checks Forge's accepted chunks and resumes only what is missing, while a deliberate sync cancellation still stops normally.

Forge now retries temporary database contention during finalization and keeps every accepted chunk safe if it is still busy. If a completion response is lost, the app can retry and receive the same stored receipt without importing the data twice.
