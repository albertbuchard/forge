Forge Companion 1.0.170 makes secure pairing faster and clearer.

The app now discovers a nearby Forge automatically when possible and starts the protected device-pairing flow directly. QR scanning and manual server entry remain available when discovery is unavailable.

Pairing still requires explicit approval from the unlocked Forge owner interface. Once approved, the Companion activates its scoped credential and renews it silently for normal daily use.
